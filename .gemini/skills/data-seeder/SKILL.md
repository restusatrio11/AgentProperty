# Skill: Property Data Seeder
Specializes in generating high-quality, realistic Indonesian property data for testing.

## Data Schema
- **Regions:** Focus on Jabodetabek or specific areas requested.
- **Pricing:** Realistic range (e.g., 500jt - 10M+).
- **Attributes:** Must include 'Lebar', 'Hadap' (Utara, Selatan, etc.), and 'Tipe Properti'.

## Logic
- Generate at least 50 records.
- Ensure a mix of 'In Stock', 'Sold Out', and 'Hidden' statuses to test filters.
